THE MAN BEHIND THE LEGACY

A simple life with an extraordinary purpose.

Abdul Sattar Edhi was a Pakistani humanitarian and philanthropist known for dedicating his life to helping people in need. His work focused on providing practical support to vulnerable communities regardless of their background.

ABOUT

A philosophy of compassion

Abdul Sattar Edhi 1928 — 2016

Edhi became known for building a humanitarian network that provided services to people facing poverty, illness, homelessness and other difficult circumstances.

His approach emphasized simplicity, accessibility and service. The organization associated with his name developed services including ambulances, shelters, orphanages, clinics and other forms of social assistance.

THE JOURNEY

Milestones of a remarkable life

Important moments associated with Edhi's humanitarian journey.

1928

Birth

Abdul Sattar Edhi was born in Bantva, Gujarat, British India.

1947

Migration to Pakistan

Following the partition of British India, Edhi migrated to Pakistan.

1951

Beginning of Organized Service

He purchased a small shop and began developing humanitarian services for people in need.

1957

Expansion of Welfare Work

His humanitarian activities expanded following the influenza epidemic in Karachi.

1986

Ramon Magsaysay Award

Edhi and his wife Bilquis Edhi received the Ramon Magsaysay Award for Public Service.

2013

International Recognition

Edhi continued to receive international recognition for his humanitarian services.

2016

Passing

Abdul Sattar Edhi passed away in Karachi on July 8, 2016.

A legacy built through service

The Edhi network became widely associated with ambulance services for people requiring emergency transportation.

Medical and health-related services formed an important part of the humanitarian network.

🍲

His welfare work also included food distribution and assistance to people experiencing hardship.

🌍

Humanitarian Values

LEGACY

The work continues beyond one lifetime.

The humanitarian organization founded by Edhi continues to provide welfare services in Pakistan. His legacy is closely connected with the idea that meaningful social change can begin with direct service to people in need.

A MESSAGE

Serve with sincerity.

The story of Abdul Sattar Edhi reminds us that humanitarian service does not always require extraordinary resources. Compassion, consistency and a willingness to help can make a meaningful difference.
